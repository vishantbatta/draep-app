Write these six rules into the Brand Book

  1. The primary CTA is solid #A85010 with its white 15 px SemiBold label (5.50 : 1; hover #9A4A0E at 6.25). White on any brighter orange fails — #B85A10 (4.66) is the ceiling, Ember only at ≥19 px bold.
  2. Orange text only at display size — ≥24 px, or ≥19 px at weight 700, in Ember #D06010. Body-size accents: Ink Navy, or #A85010 at 5.50 : 1.
  3. Ember #D06010 is the non-text orange — tick dividers, the Tape Timeline, dashed in-progress borders and focus rings (3.6–3.9 : 1).
  4. Success is #15803D. Warning yellow appears only as a background under Ink Navy text; standalone warning text is #A16207.
  5. A 15 px SemiBold label is normal text — every button, chip and badge holds 4.5 : 1, not the 3 : 1 large-text bar.
  6. Define Tape Silver in §04. §06 uses it for in-progress borders but the palette never assigns it a hex — an unspecified color cannot be audited.
The brand survives intact: every fix stays inside the existing hue families — most are shades the palette already contains, promoted to named roles. Nothing changes but lightness; everything changes for the 1 in 4 users who need it.
