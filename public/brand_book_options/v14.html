Couture Noir — the maison fits one body at a time

Haute couture forgot
it was always about you.

The houses put one dress on one body and called it art — then sold everyone else the copy. We keep the art and skip the copy: every piece is the piece, cut for the person wearing it. Walk out and make the street your runway.

The collection — four looks, one house

Custom-stitched is cool

Rarity is the oldest luxury. Nothing is rarer than a garment that exists once, on you.

Look № 2Duty

Beautify the world

A couture client commissions beauty for a room. You wear yours into every room in the city.

Look № 3Space

A safe space to be fitted

Our atelier turns no silhouette away. The mirror here flatters every draft of you.

Look № 4Your way

Cut your way

You are the client, the muse, and the direction. However you want the world beautified, the scissors follow.
