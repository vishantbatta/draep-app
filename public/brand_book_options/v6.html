Direction 06 — The White Wall
Gallery Minimal

Museums don't hang
multiples.

Anything made a thousand times goes to the warehouse. Anything made once gets the white wall. Custom-stitched clothing is you, exhibited — and the world is a better gallery for it.

Four works, permanent collection
No. 1

Custom-stitched is cool

Singular work carries singular prestige. The rarest thing you can wear is something that only fits one person — you.

No. 2

Beautify the world

Every piece you hang on yourself is hung in public. Curate with care; the street is everyone's gallery.

No. 3

A safe space

No critic at this door. Whatever you bring, the white wall receives it without comment.

No. 4

Shown your way

The artist decides the frame, the light, the angle. Beautify the world exactly as you intend.
