Direction 19 — The Keepsake Book
Scrapbook Stitch — everything good is taped in by hand

Nobody scrapbooks
the boring days.
Neither do we.

A scrapbook only holds things that happened once — a ticket, a note, a ribbon. Your closet should work the same way: garments stitched for one moment and one person, then worn out into the world until the whole album is beautiful.

Four pages, taped in forever
Page one, with love

Custom-stitched is cool

Scrapbooks keep the singular stuff and bin the duplicates. So do the cool kids. One garment, one person, no reprints.

Page two, the big one

Beautify the world

Every day you walk outside, you tape a new page into the world's album. Make it one people smile at.

Page three, soft corner

A safe space

No page in this book gets graded. Bring the weird ticket stubs — they're usually the best ones.

Page four, yours to fill

Arranged your way

You choose what gets taped where. However you want the world beautified, we cut and keep to your layout.
