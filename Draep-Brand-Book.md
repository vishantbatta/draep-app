## 01 · Brand Foundation

Everything Draep does starts with a measurement — of your body, your style, and a promise kept.

**Mission — Simplify custom tailoring through technology**
Seamless design selection, accurate digital measurements, transparent pricing, and consistent quality across every order.

**Vision — The most trusted tailoring ecosystem**
Redefine the global tailoring industry by connecting customers, tailors, and fashion through precision, innovation, and craftsmanship.

### Values

1. **Precision** — Every measurement matters — taken by SOP, stored forever.
2. **Trust** — Transparent pricing, written SLAs, the Protection Policy.
3. **Craftsmanship** — Skilled tailors celebrated and paid fairly, never hidden.
4. **Innovation** — Technology that simplifies a fragmented industry.
5. **Customer First** — Bespoke made easy, accessible, and enjoyable.
6. **Consistency** — The same high standard in every home, every city.

### Brand Personality — *The Master Tailor*

As exact as an engineer, as welcoming as a good host. Draep anticipates the fit, protects the fabric, and keeps every promise — so dressing for the moments that matter feels effortless.

Traits: **Precise · Warm · Dependable · Craft-proud**

## 03 · Visual Identity — Warm Precision

The exactness of an engineering tool, the warmth of an Indian home. The measuring tape is the source of every shape, pattern, and motion.

- **Color Physics** — Deep Ink Navy carries trust and calm; a warm Draep Orange tape gradient brings energy and celebration. Together they are the brand handshake — never one without the other. Orange is the spice, not the base.
- **Type Hierarchy** — Poppins' rounded geometry echoes the lowercase wordmark; Inter keeps dense order and catalogue UI legible; IBM Plex Mono makes every measurement look measured.
- **Spatial Logic** — Soft, tape-like curves everywhere — 12px cards, pill buttons. Ticks become dividers, the rivet becomes the end-dot of every timeline, the alpha loop frames imagery.

## 08 · Using the Mark

The measuring tape looped into an alpha — treat it like a finished garment: clean edges, room to breathe, worn on the right backgrounds.

- **Clear space & size** — Keep a margin equal to the height of the wordmark's "a" on every side. Never render the symbol below 24px on screen (8mm print) or the primary lockup below 96px wide.
- **Backgrounds** — Full-color logo on Warm Sand, Chalk White, or Mist Navy. Reversed logo on Ink Navy or a navy photo overlay of 40%+. Never on busy or light images.
- **File formats** — SVG for web & app, transparent PNG at 2×/3× for raster, PDF/EPS for print & embroidery, and single-color navy/white for stamps and one-color work.

### ✅ Do / ❌ Don't

**Do**
- Keep the full-color mark on sand, white, or mist navy
- Use the reversed lockup on navy or dark photos
- Respect the clear-space margin on every side
- Lead with the symbol alone for icons & avatars
- Scale proportionally, always from a corner

**Don't**
- Recolor the tape or flatten its gradient
- Set the wordmark in another typeface or uppercase
- Rotate, outline, add shadows to, or stretch the lockup
- Place the full-color logo on orange or busy photos
- Separate the ticks from the tape

## 09 · Physical & Merch Design

Draep in the real world — uniforms, packaging, and the tape itself. Tailored, not printed-on: embroidery and woven labels over loud graphics.

- **Tailored, not printed-on** — Favor embroidery, woven labels, debossing, and tonal prints. The brand is craft, not merch.
- **Navy + sand base** — Keep garments and packaging in Ink Navy, Warm Sand, or natural cotton; use Draep Orange only as thread — stitching, tape trim, a single tab.
- **The tape is the signature** — A measuring-tape trim, tick marks, or the alpha loop reads as Draep instantly — use it as edging, seams, and zip pulls.

### Apparel
- **Style Captain polo** — navy body, orange symbol on the chest, woven wordmark at the back collar
- **T-shirt** — sand or white, tonal tape-loop print, one orange line: "Measured for you"
- **Apron** — navy canvas, orange tape trim, tick-divided pockets
- **Cap** — symbol front-center, wordmark on the back strap
- **Tote / garment bag** — natural cotton, navy wordmark, one orange handle band

### Packaging & collateral
- **Garment box** — sand board, navy wordmark, orange tape "seal", tick-pattern interior
- **Woven label** — navy label, orange symbol, order ID in Plex Mono
- **Business card** — navy stock, reversed logo, orange-painted edge
- **Branded tape** — sand tape, navy numerals, orange rivet end, wordmark at 0

### ✅ Do / ❌ Don't

**Do**
- Embroider or deboss the mark
- Keep to one accent color
- Respect clear space on fabric
- Use durable, natural materials

**Don't**
- Stretch the logo across a whole chest
- Use neon or off-palette colors
- Combine multiple large marks
- Print the gradient on textured fabric — use solid orange thread
